# Diagnóstico Financiero Local

## Core Purpose
An offline-first, local-only desktop application (built with Tauri + React) designed to provide users with a secure and fast environment to diagnose, analyze, and simulate their personal or professional finances. 

## Target Audience
Individuals and professionals who need a reliable, private tool to track transactions, analyze budget matrices, and simulate financial scenarios without relying on cloud storage or external servers.

## Key Principles
- **Privacy First:** 100% local data. No cloud sync, no external tracking.
- **Immediate Value:** The user should see their financial health snapshot (Estado de Resultados) immediately upon opening the dashboard.
- **High Utility:** Designed for dense data consumption without feeling overwhelming.

## Capabilities
- Transaction tracking and categorization.
- Budget matrix analysis (Inicial vs. Mejorado).
- Financial simulation and projections.
- Dual Income Statement (Estado de Resultados) visualization.
